In development · pilot for a small group of shops

The back office for 3D printing shops.

ERPrinter helps small 3D printing businesses that sell their own designs keep production, materials and orders in one place.

What it does

Print cost estimates

Estimate the cost of each print from material, machine time and your own parameters.

Materials inventory

Track filament and resin stock and see what each production run will use.

Production queue

Turn orders into production jobs and follow them until they ship.

Orders and basic finance

Keep order status, receivables and payables linked to each order.

Etsy integration

Sellers who also sell on Etsy can connect their own shop, so that:

  • their Etsy orders appear in the production queue without retyping;
  • after they confirm a stock change, the available quantity of their own listings is updated.

ERPrinter only acts on the shop of the seller who authorized it. It does not message buyers, create listings or replace any Etsy feature. It is intended for sellers of items they design and produce themselves, in line with Etsy's Creativity Standards.

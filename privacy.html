Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft of November 2, 2026

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we") provides ERPrinter (the "Service") to businesses ("Customers"). This policy explains what data we process, why, where it is stored, for how long, and the choices you have.

1. Roles

  • For data about Customers' own users (name, e-mail, login activity), we act as a controller.
  • For business data that Customers enter or import (products, inventory, orders, production, finance) and for data retrieved from connected marketplaces, we act as a processor / service provider on the Customer's behalf. For Etsy data, we act as a service provider to the Etsy seller who connected the shop.

2. Data we collect

Category Examples Source
Account data Name, business e-mail, company name, role, login timestamps You, at sign-up and when inviting users
Subscription data AWS account ID, AWS Marketplace license identifier, product code, subscription status AWS Marketplace, when you subscribe
Business data Products, materials, stock, orders, production records, receivables and payables You
Marketplace data Shop name and ID; orders/receipts (items, variations, quantities, dates, status); listings and quantities Marketplaces you connect (e.g., Etsy), within the permissions you approve
Security and audit data Security-relevant events (logins, permission changes, connections, exports, deletions), IP address, user agent The Service

We do not collect payment card or bank account details. We do not store buyer e-mail addresses, and we do not store buyer names, shipping addresses, payment e-mail addresses, buyer messages or gift messages received from marketplaces: these fields are discarded when orders are imported.

3. How we use data

To provide and secure the Service; to sync orders and stock with marketplaces you connect; to verify your subscription with AWS Marketplace; to provide support; to detect and investigate security incidents; and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or use marketplace data to train AI models.

4. Where data is stored

Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the region selected for your account: United States (us-east-1) or European Union — Spain (eu-south-2). Customer business data stays in the selected region. Subscription records are processed in us-east-1 because AWS Marketplace services operate there. [To be completed after legal review.]

5. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AWS KMS-managed keys, with scheduled rotation); tenant isolation enforced in the database; authentication required for all access; least-privilege access for our staff; tamper-protected audit logs.

6. Sharing

We share data only with: - Subprocessors that host or operate the Service: Amazon Web Services, Inc. and affiliates [To be completed after legal review.]. - Marketplaces you connect, only to perform actions you request (for example, updating listing quantities on Etsy). - Authorities, when required by law.

7. Retention

Data Retention
Business data While your subscription is active; deleted within 30 (proposed) days after account closure, unless you request earlier deletion
Marketplace tokens and marketplace data Deleted within 30 (proposed) days after you disconnect the marketplace or close the account
Audit logs At least 1 year (required by AWS Marketplace), then deleted
Backups Rolling backups kept for 35 (proposed) days; deleted data disappears from backups when they expire
Subscription records As long as required for billing and legal obligations

8. Backups

Encrypted backups are taken automatically in the same region as the data and are used only to restore the Service.

9. Your rights and requests

Customers can export and delete their data from the Service or by writing to privacy@erprinter.com. Individuals in the EU and in U.S. states with privacy laws may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of their personal data; requests about data a Customer controls are forwarded to that Customer.

10. Security incidents

We notify affected Customers without undue delay. For Etsy data, we notify Etsy and the affected seller within 24 hours of discovery; a suspected compromise of our Etsy API credentials is reported to Etsy immediately. Report a suspected incident at security@erprinter.com.

11. Changes

We will notify Customers of material changes before they take effect.
